I believe education should inspire curiosity, critical thinking, and a genuine love for learning. My classes favor exploration over memorization: students are encouraged to ask "how" and "why," and every hands-on activity is tied to a clear learning outcome. For example, students work through self-guided activities to tell multiplexing apart from demultiplexing, and build a reliable communication protocol through iterative problem-solving.

As AI increasingly automates coding, I emphasize computational thinking, problem-solving, and the ability to critically evaluate AI-generated code.
